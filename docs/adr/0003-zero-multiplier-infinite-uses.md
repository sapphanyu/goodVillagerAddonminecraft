# Zero Price Multiplier and Saturated Trade Uses

We decided to set `price_multiplier: 0.0` and `max_uses: 999999` across all trade offers, rather than relying on vanilla workstation restocking mechanics or temporary discount tags. This eliminates supply-and-demand price inflation and completely prevents out-of-stock lockouts, providing stable, permanent trade pricing and non-stop trading convenience without waiting for daily villager restock routines.
