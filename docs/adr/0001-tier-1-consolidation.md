# Tier 1 Trade Consolidation

We decided to consolidate all villager trade offers directly into Tier 1 (Novice) with zero required experience, rather than keeping the vanilla 5-tier progression with reduced XP thresholds. This completely eliminates the need for players to grind XP or cycle trades to unlock high-tier items (such as Diamond Gear and Master Enchantments), delivering immediate 100% catalog access upon opening the trade UI.
